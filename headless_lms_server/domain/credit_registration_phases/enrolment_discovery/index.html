The enrolment-discovery phase: who the study registry says is on the course.

One listing unparks the registrations of people we already have a link for, links the few whose registry address is an address one of our accounts has proved it controls, and claims an account-linking mail for everybody else. That middle branch is terminal, never a filter: the population the linking mail exists to reach is the people whose two addresses differ, and every fast-track outcome other than a link falls through to the mail.
