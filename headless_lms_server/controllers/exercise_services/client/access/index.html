The given exercises with a slide all of whose tasks are client-capable, the slides client_tasks_from_slide serves. Unlike that, this selects no slide for the user, so it also answers for exercises whose chapter has not opened.

A slide’s tasks in the client shape, or none when the client cannot answer the slide. Shared by the list and single-exercise views so their visibility rules cannot drift apart. Model solutions are stripped unless reveal_model_solution; the list view never reveals them.

The course’s chapters, split into the ones open to the user and the ones that have not opened. A user who may view the course’s material before it opens, such as its staff, gets every chapter as open, as the single-exercise and submit routes already let them answer those.
