Module exercise_answer_snapshots 

Source
Expand description

Ungraded copies of a student’s answer: a paste to share, or a backup kept before local changes are discarded.

Deliberately a table of their own rather than a kind of exercise_slide_submissions row: every query that grades, counts tries, awards points, picks peer reviews or computes completion reads that table, and none of them has to remember to skip snapshots.
