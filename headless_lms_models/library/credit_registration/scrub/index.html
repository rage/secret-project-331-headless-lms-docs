STUDENT_NUMBER_RE 🔒
Student-number-shaped digit runs. The id shapes come first because the regex crate has no lookaround: leftmost-first alternation is the only way to say “digits not part of an id”. The word boundaries keep the digit branch off longer numbers such as millisecond timestamps.
