Module enrolment_check_schedule 

Source
Expand description

When a row waiting for an enrolment is checked next.

Each group has a ladder of offsets from its anchor. The next check is always the first rung after now, so a row that missed rungs (an outage, a check brought forward) gets one catch-up check rather than one per missed rung, and a row past its last rung has stopped.
