Module course_unit_poll_slots 

Source
Expand description

When a partially registered row next looks for its course unit attainment. Sisu creates those in a nightly batch, so a row polls once after it, on the morning slot, and again on the noon slot if the morning poll came too early. A row that first shows its assessment item attainment during the day waits for the next morning.
