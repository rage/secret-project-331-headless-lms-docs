Module moocfi_report 

Source
Expand description

Telling mooc.fi about each registration we made of its completions, once per registration.

mooc.fi keeps every registration as history and does not skip a repeated post, so each registration is posted only under a lease, and each post follows a lookup that finds a registration whose answer was lost, by our organization and its registration date. Which registrations are reported at all is moocfi_reportable_registrations.
