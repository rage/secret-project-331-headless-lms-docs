Module ai_matching 

Source
Expand description

Inputs and answers of the AI that suggests counterparts from titles, names and content: what is asked about one chapter of this course and one other course, and how its answer maps back.

Structs§

AiKnownPair
Pages or exercises of the two courses that are fixed already: linked or copies of each other.
AiMatchingAnswer
The AI’s answer to one AiMatchingTask.
AiMatchingItem
A page or exercise the AI is asked to match.
AiMatchingTask
One AI request: the unlinked pages and exercises of one chapter number (or of the pages outside chapters) in this course and in one other course.
AiPick
One pick in the AI’s answer. Ids are strings so that a mistyped id is caught here rather than failing the whole answer.
AiPicks
The AI’s picks in one other course by this course’s item id: None is an explicit “no counterpart”. Items without an entry were not answered and keep the heuristic suggestion.
DroppedPicks
Picks of an answer that AiPicks::add_answer dropped.
