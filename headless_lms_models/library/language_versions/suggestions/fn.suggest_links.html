Suggested counterparts for course_id’s pages and exercises in every other course of the snapshot (load it with super::snapshot::load_with_candidates to include courses before a join). chapter_number limits the rows and unmatched items to one chapter number. ai picks outrank every heuristic but links and copy history (see super::ai_matching); the caller sets ai_status.
