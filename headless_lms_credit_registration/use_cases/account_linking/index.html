Module account_linking 

Source
Expand description

The account-linking action an admin or a teacher sets off by hand: re-running the linking mail send path for one person on one course. Not a phase: no schedule and no allowance, and no breaker learns from its calls, so one click cannot trip the workers’.

The addresses come from the study registry rather than the ledger, and the claim goes through [claim_linking_mails], so the caps and dedup guard apply exactly as they do to the worker.
