Module rate_limit 

Source
Expand description

How much one worker process may ask of each rate-limited Suotar endpoint.

One token bucket per endpoint, in memory like the circuit breaker: every endpoint is called from one process only. After its own failures, or when the breaker trips or closes again, an endpoint drops to a tenth of its rate and doubles back every five healthy minutes.

Keyed by scope like the breaker, so a test’s scoped ticks never spend what the live workers or another test may send.
